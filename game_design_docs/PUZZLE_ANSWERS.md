# Puzzle Answer Key

This is a spoiler reference for the currently coded puzzle answers.

## Stage 1 - Pilot's Cabin

### Message Screen

- Match `il` to `I / me`.
- Match `al` to `you`.

### Planet Posters

- Click `rom` on both planet labels:
  - `desa rom`
  - `fanna rom`

### Crew Roster

- Drop `ramde` on the Pilot Role / `ramdenu` card.
- Drop `derbe` on the Medic Role / `derbenu` card.

### Liquid Dispenser

- Choose `doda`.

### Bed Label

- Type a translation that includes both ideas:
  - `you` or `your`
  - `planet` or `world`
- Example accepted answer: `you planet?`

### Cabin Door Lock

- Build: `op- gane`

## Stage 2 - Clinic

### Clinic Sign

- Click `mar` on both doorway signs:
  - `derbemar`
  - `ramdemar`
- Then click `derbe` on the clinic sign `derbemar`.

### Medic Screen

- Click `il` for the medic pointing to themself.
- Click `uk` in `derbeuk` for the completed-entry ending.

### Treatment Wall

- Drag the treatment record toward `me-sondy` until Keep Clue is enabled.

### Medicine Shelf

- Select the germ label: `carda`.
- Build the anti-virus bottle label: `pua- carda`.

### Refill Machine

- Build: `ya- moll`

## Stage 3 - Lab

### Lab Sign

- Click `mar` on both room signs:
  - `derbemar`
  - `crutamar`
- Then click `cruta` on the lab sign `crutamar`.

### Manufacturing Machine

- First batch: `moll puacarda`
- Refill cargo rack: `ya- moll puacarda`

### Destination Map

- Build: `doda rom`

### Communication Door

- Build: `il kume`

### Words Before The Bridge

- Complete or dismiss the vocabulary review to unlock the Bridge / Comms room.

## Stage 4 - Bridge

### Commander Channel

- "I am healed": `il derbe -uk`
- "I am flying to Earth so Earth can be healed": `il ramde derbe doda rom`
- "You fly the antiviral medicine to Earth": `al ramde pua- carda doda rom`
